Neckar Saturday

Make your own way to the Mercedes-Benz Museum after it opens at 09:00, meet everyone for lunch at Bertha's at 12:45, then take a two-hour river cruise on the MS Wilhelma and visit Dock Snyder — with taxis to Augustiner whenever the mood is right.

The day, hour by hour

  • 09:00

    Mercedes-Benz Museum opens

    Make your own way there and arrive whenever it suits you. The museum is open 09:00–18:00; everyone meets at Bertha's at 12:45. Nine floors and the audio guide are included. People sharing VVS GroupDayTickets must travel together; anyone going alone needs their own valid ticket.

    15 × €15 = €225
  • 12:45

    Lunch at Bertha's (in the museum)

    The fixed meeting point for the whole group, with zero transfer time before heading towards the boat. Details below — excluded from the budget as requested.

  • 13:30

    To the Wilhelma boat pier

    Flat ~35 min walk along the Neckar (cross at Rosensteinbrücke; pier is on Neckartalstraße at the Wilhelma zoo entrance). Anyone not keen to walk: 3–4 taxis, ~10 min, roughly €12–15 per car. Boarding from ~14:15 — you'll arrive with a comfortable buffer.

  • 14:30

    MS Wilhelma — Lazy Saturday Afternoon Tour

    Every Saturday 14:30–16:30 (season runs March–October): Stuttgart → past Max-Eyth-See, through the Hofen lock, below the steep vineyards to Aldingen and back. Drinks and cake sold on board (Kaffee & Kuchen package €8.90 if you want it).

    15 × €27.90 + ticket fee ≈ €433.50
  • 16:35

    U14 to Max-Eyth-See

    The Wilhelma tram stop is right by the pier. U14 direction Mühlhausen, ~10 min to Max-Eyth-See, then ~10 min on foot around the lake to the peninsula. Covered by the morning's day tickets.

  • 17:00

    Dock Snyder

    Beach-bar beer garden on the Max-Eyth-See peninsula: sand, loungers, hammocks, beer on tap, food trucks, vineyard views — and rowing/pedal boat rental on the lake if anyone wants a second round on the water. Open from around midday, March–October, weather permitting.

  • Flexible

    Taxis to Bad Cannstatt

    Order enough taxis or minivans when the group feels ready to move on. The road journey to the Kursaal is about 4.8 km and normally takes 10–15 minutes.

  • 19:00

    Augustiner Biergarten

    Dinner and Augustiner from the tap beside the historic Kursaal. The Saturday beer garden normally runs until 23:00 in good weather. Official website.

  • late

    Home

    U2 from Kursaal runs back through the city centre; get off at Rotebühlplatz for the short walk to the Maritim. The day tickets remain valid until 7:00 the next morning.

Worth asking when you book the boat: the cruise sails right past Dock Snyder, and there is a landing stage at Max-Eyth-See. Ask NeckarFlair whether your group of 15 may disembark there on the return leg (~16:05) — if yes, you step off almost at the beer garden door, skip the U14 hop and gain extra time before the taxis.

Lunch suggestion (not in the budget)

Bertha's Bar & Restaurant, on the ground floor of the museum itself — Swabian dishes (Maultaschen, lentils & Spätzle) plus burgers, pinsa and pasta. Restaurant open Tue–Sun 11:30–16:00, with a faster bistro/deli alongside. Reserve a table for 15 at 12:45 and ideally pre-order: reservierung@gastro-mbm.de / +49 711 66419-613. Reckon on roughly €15–25 a head plus drinks.

Book before you go

  • Museum group ticket — register 15 people in advance; the museum opens at 09:00 and everyone makes their own way there (group rate €15 pp from 10 visitors): museum-ticket.mercedes-benz.com
  • Bertha's — table for 15 at 12:45, mention the 14:30 boat so mains land quickly: reservierung@gastro-mbm.de
  • MS Wilhelma cruise — book 15 seats for the Lazy Saturday Afternoon Tour at neckarflair.de; for a group this size email info.wilhelma@neckarflair.de and ask about (a) hopping off at Max-Eyth-See and (b) their minimum-booking policy — Neckar-Käpt'n reserves the right to cancel under-booked sailings and issue vouchers, so reconfirm the week before.
  • Dock Snyder — ask whether they can hold a table cluster for 15 around 17:00: docksnyder.de/contact. It's open-air and weather-dependent.
  • Taxis — order enough cars or minivans from Dock Snyder when there is stemning for det.
  • Augustiner Biergarten — reserve for 15 people at 19:00: official website / +49 711 99777242.
  • Morning of — coordinate three travelling groups if using the 3 GroupDayTickets; anyone travelling separately needs their own valid VVS ticket.

Plan B — rain or a cancelled sailing

If the cruise is cancelled (weather or under-booking), take the day at an easier pace: three full hours in the museum, a long lunch at Bertha's, then U-Bahn/taxis to Max-Eyth-See and rent rowing and pedal boats at Dock Snyder's own jetty — you still get on the water, just on the lake, for a few euros per boat instead of €28 a head. In steady rain, skip Dock Snyder and go to the Kursaal area early; Augustiner is also weather-dependent, but the adjacent Kursaal Restaurant provides an indoor fallback.
